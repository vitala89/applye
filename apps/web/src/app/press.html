Boilerplate

Applye is an open-source, local-first desktop app for an AI-powered job search. It scores job descriptions against a candidate's profile, tailors the CV per role, drafts cover letters and follow-ups, and tracks the pipeline - entirely on the user's machine, with no account and no telemetry. Users bring the AI they already pay for; every submission remains a human decision. Built by Vitalii Kasap during his own job search in Germany, Applye treats the German market as first-class. It is MIT-licensed and free forever.
