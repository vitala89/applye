Boilerplate

Applye is an open-source, local-first desktop app for an AI-powered job search. It scores job descriptions against a candidate's profile, tailors the CV per role, drafts cover letters and follow-ups, and tracks the pipeline - entirely on the user's machine, with no account and no telemetry. Users bring the AI they already pay for; every submission remains a human decision. It works in any country, and handles the paperwork that differs between them - including the German Eigenbemühungen report, a legacy of the author's own job search in Germany. It is MIT-licensed and free.
