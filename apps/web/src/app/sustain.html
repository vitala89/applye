Sustain

Free. Here is how that works.

Applye is MIT-licensed with no paid tier, no premium features, no waitlist, and no plan to sell your data - there is no account to attach data to in the first place. The author has paid work for income; Applye exists because he needed it. Sustainability comes from the community, voluntarily.

What keeps the project alive

  • Star the repository. Visibility is oxygen for an open-source tool - it is genuinely the single most helpful free action. Star Applye on GitHub.
  • Tell one person. Someone in your circle is job hunting right now. Send them the link.
  • Contribute. Issues, docs, translations (the UI ships in six languages), and code. Start with CONTRIBUTING.md.
  • Sponsor, if you want to. Voluntary patronage via GitHub Sponsors funds deeper focus on the project. It buys development time, never features - sponsors get gratitude, not influence.

What we will never do

  • Charge for the app or gate features behind a tier.
  • Sell, share, or even hold your data - it never reaches us.
  • Add ads, telemetry, or growth hacks that spam recruiters on your behalf.
  • Resell AI tokens with a margin. You bring your own keys and pay your provider directly.

If the model ever needs to change, it changes in the open, in the changelog, before anything ships.
